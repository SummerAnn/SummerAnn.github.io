Game Features

Blend the real world with digital adventure through cutting-edge AR technology

Interactive Map

Explore a dynamic map filled with quests, challenges, and hidden treasures scattered throughout real-world locations around you.

AR Scanning

Use your camera to scan real-world objects and unlock virtual rewards, discover hidden creatures, and complete AR challenges.

Epic Quests

Embark on location-based quests with engaging storylines, challenging objectives, and exclusive rewards for adventurous explorers.

Collect & Earn

Build your collection of rare items, achievements, and exclusive badges as you explore and complete adventures.

Social Adventures

Team up with friends for cooperative quests, compete on leaderboards, and share your discoveries with the community.

Dynamic World

Experience a game world that responds to real-world conditions including time of day, weather, and seasonal events.
