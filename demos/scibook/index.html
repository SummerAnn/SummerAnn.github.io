On the Asymptotic Convergence of Gradient Descent in Overparameterized Neural Networks

We present a rigorous analysis of gradient descent dynamics in the infinite-width limit, establishing novel convergence bounds that improve upon existing results by a factor of O(log n). Our approach leverages recent advances in mean-field theory and provides explicit characterization of the loss landscape geometry near saddle points.
